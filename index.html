PROJECT INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

Transforming F1 Data Into Championship Intelligence

This project leverages the rich historical and telemetry data from Formula 1 to build a comprehensive analytics platform. Using Tableau's powerful visual engine, each metric and championship trend is turned into interactive, actionable insights.

Global Race Overview
Interactive world map tracking Grand Prix host countries, circuit telemetry, and driver country performance.
Driver Performance Metrics
Lap times, qualifications, title gaps, comparing telemetry benchmarks across champion eras.
Constructor Championship Battle
Points trajectories, constructor standings, engine supplier reliability, and budget efficiency.
Pit Stop & Strategy Analytics
Tire compound degradation modeling, undercut vs overcut performance, and pit lane duration analytics.
Championship Prediction Modeling
Predictive telemetry models forecasting constructor and driver championship probability outcomes.
